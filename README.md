This project is a game-like farming simulator exploring crop growth under various environmental conditions.

main features:
  - Seasonal changes of weather.
  - Environmental conditions influence crop growth and state.
  - Moisture, quality, and nutrients of the soil.
  - The level of soil moisture affected by rainfall and evaporation.
  - Nutrients are spent by crops when they are harvested.
  - Players can water crops and influence soil moisture.
  - Manages crop seeds and farm finances.
  - Starts/pauses automatic simulation.
  - Sets simulation speed.
  - Resets the farm and simulation.
  - Tracks crop growing process and state.
  - Tracks average soil moisture level.
  - Shows total harvest and crop yield.
  - Tracks farm data.
how to use:
1. Open the demo link
2. Selects a crop to plant.
3. Clicks the empty farm field to plant a crop.
4. Observes weather, soil moisture and crop state.
5. Waters crops, if needed.
6. Advances time manually or starts automatic simulation.
7. Harvests crops when they are fully grown.
8. Uses harvest yield to earn money and buy new seeds.
